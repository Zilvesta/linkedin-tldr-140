What gets downloaded?

Chrome downloads Gemini Nano, its built-in AI model, directly from Google. This extension contains no model and no downloadable code of its own.

  • Shared by all of Chrome, not owned by this extension — if something else already fetched it, this is instant.
  • One time only. Find it at chrome://components under "Optimization Guide On Device Model".
  • Chrome requires ~22 GB free disk before it will start. The model itself is much smaller.
  • Once it's there, summaries run on your machine. No posts are ever sent anywhere.

Don't want it? Set the engine to Fast local — it needs no download, works on any machine, and handles every language.

Summaries are generated on your device. No account, no server, no tracking.
